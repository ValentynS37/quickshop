# BADS AI Administrator — Commercial Summary

## First sale

Sell one controlled workflow, not an unlimited AI employee.

### Founding Pilot

- 10,000 UAH implementation;
- 4,900 UAH/month managed service;
- one workflow;
- one KPI;
- synthetic or approved data;
- human approval;
- evidence trail;
- anonymized case study and testimonial.

### Standard Pilot

- 25,000 UAH implementation;
- 7,500 UAH/month managed service;
- up to two workflows;
- one or two integrations;
- evidence trail and approval queue;
- results report and rollout roadmap.

## Primary differentiator

Other automation services generate outputs. BADS records why the output was generated, which source and policy were used, what the model did, what risks were flagged, and who approved the external action.

## Revenue path

1. Implementation fee.
2. Monthly management fee.
3. Additional workflow and integration fees.
4. Evidence and compliance module.
5. Multi-client BADS Agent Control Center subscription.
6. Separate enterprise TrustOS licensing and on-prem deployments.

## Gate for platform investment

Do not build the full subscription platform until:

- three pilots have run;
- at least two clients pay monthly;
- one workflow is repeated across multiple clients;
- support and API costs are measured;
- clients actively use evidence search or approvals.
